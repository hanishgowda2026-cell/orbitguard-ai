PROJECT INFORMATION

About ORBITGUARD AI

Machine-learning research prototype for forecasting final conjunction risk using historical ESA data.

How It Works

ESA's Collision Avoidance Challenge contains repeated Conjunction Data Messages (CDMs) for each encounter. We select the latest CDM available at least two days before closest approach and train a regression model to estimate the final recorded log10 collision probability.

We split by encounter event, not by CDM row, to avoid placing the same encounter in both training and validation. The displayed risk category is an educational UI convention, not an ESA operational threshold.
